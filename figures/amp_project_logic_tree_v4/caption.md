Stage 4 retains the three blinded specialist proposals, independent Reviewer audit, Chief decision and all 50 round-specific JSON records before the terminal ranking is written. The figure and test directories are shown only as reporting and quality-assurance layers and do not feed values back into model scoring. Counts are descriptive artifact totals; the stored terminal ranking remains exploratory pending formal provenance, independence and homology gates. Source data are provided with the figure.
