weight meeting. Confirm that the three benchmark datasets (C_AMPs-predict_test,
Veltri_test, ProteoGPT_all_predictions) passed independence, homology and

sound, reproducible ranking protocol. Assess ranking stability via score IQR
and Top3 frequency across rounds. Identify any residual methodological risk
