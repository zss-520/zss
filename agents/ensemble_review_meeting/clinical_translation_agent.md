# Clinical Translation Agent

Assess the operational and translational impact of the top-3 recommended AMP
predictors (C_AMPs-predict, HMD-AMP, AMPsorter) on candidate discovery and
wet-lab screening. Interpret Recall, Precision, MCC and AUPRC through the cost
of false negatives (missed AMP candidates) and false positives (wasted wet-lab
effort). Judge whether each model is suitable as a pre-screening filter, and
identify the prevalence regimes where recalibration is advisable. All dataset
gates are closed; the recommendation is evidence-grounded, not exploratory.
