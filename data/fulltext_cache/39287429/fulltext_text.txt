E-mail: novick@saturn.med.nyu.edu . 10 2004 70 10 17311 6076 6085 24 11 2003 30 4 2004 01 10 2004 16 10 2004 09 03 2025 Copyright © 2004, American Society for Microbiology 2004 Our understanding of staphylococcal pathogenesis depends on reliable genetic tools for gene expression analysis and tracing of bacteria. Here, we have developed and evaluated a series of novel versatile Escherichia coli -staphylococcal shuttle vectors based on PCR-generated interchangeable cassettes. Advantages of our module system include the use of (i) staphylococcal low-copy-number, high-copy-number, thermosensitive and theta replicons and selectable markers (choice of erythromycin, tetracycline, chloramphenicol, kanamycin, or spectinomycin); (ii) an E. coli replicon and selectable marker (ampicillin); and (iii) a staphylococcal phage fragment that allows high-frequency transduction and an SaPI fragment that allows site-specific integration into the Staphylococcus aureus chromosome. The staphylococcal cadmium-inducible P cad -cadC and constitutive P promoters were designed and analyzed in transcriptional fusions to the staphylococcal β-lactamase blaZ blaZ , the Vibrio fischeri luxAB , and the Aequorea victoria green fluorescent protein reporter genes. The modular design of the vector system provides great flexibility and variety.
