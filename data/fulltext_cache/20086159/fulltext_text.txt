Tel: +1 402 559 4176; Fax: +1 402 559 4651; Email: gwang@unmc.edu 1 1 2004 32 Database issue 9036 D590 D592 11 8 2003 20 8 2003 3 9 2003 01 01 2004 08 01 2004 22 12 2010 Copyright © 2004 Oxford University Press 2004 An antimicrobial peptide database (APD) has been established based on an extensive literature search. It contains detailed information for 525 peptides (498 antibacterial, 155 antifungal, 28 antiviral and 18 antitumor). APD provides interactive interfaces for peptide query, prediction and design. It also provides statistical data for a select group of or all the peptides in the database. Peptide information can be searched using keywords such as peptide name, ID, length, net charge, hydrophobic percentage, key residue, unique sequence motif, structure and activity. APD is a useful tool for studying the structure–function relationship of antimicrobial peptides.
